Category 01

Bespoke Blazers & Dinner Jackets

Engineered for effortless distinction. Whether in lush velvet for black-tie galas or unstructured hopsack wool for smart-casual wear, each jacket features hand-rolled lapels, roped shoulders, and cupro silk inner lining.

  • Classic Peak, Notch, and Shawl Satin Lapels
  • Hand-stitched Pick-Stitching along edges
  • Genuine Mother-of-Pearl & Horn Buttons
Black Tie Luxury

The Evening Dinner Jacket

Black velvet with grosgrain silk peak lapel and dual back vents.

Category 02

Hand-Tailored Bespoke Trousers

A jacket is only as commanding as the trousers anchoring it. Cut with high-rise Hollywood waistbands, deep forward pleats, and extended waistband closures with brass side adjusters for a belt-free silhouette.

  • Single or Double Forward Pleats with sharp crease
  • Gurkha Side Buckles & Suspender Buttons
  • Turn-up Cuffs (1.75" / 2") or Clean Minimal Break
Sartorial Cut

The High-Rise Flannel Trouser

Dual forward pleats, coin pocket, and 2-inch cuffs crafted in English flannel.

Category 03

Custom Monogrammed Dress Shirts

Constructed with 22 stitches per inch using two-ply 100% Egyptian Giza Cotton. Hand-turned collars with removable brass stays and custom embroidered monograms on the left cuff or chest.

  • Cutaway, Semi-Spread, and Tuxedo Wingtips
  • French Double Cuffs or Curved Barrel Cuffs
  • Custom Hand-Stitched Monogram Included
100% Giza Cotton

Egyptian Giza Twill

Mother-of-pearl buttons with hand-sewn crow's foot stitching.

Category 04

Royal Prince Suit & Ceremonial Attire

The pinnacle of Indian royal tailoring. Featuring aristocratic Bandhgala collars, structured high-standing lapels, antique metallic buttons, and intricate tone-on-tone embroidery designed for royal groom celebrations.

  • High-neck Bandhgala Prince Silhouette
  • Zardozi & Resham Hand-Embroidered Accents
  • Pure Raw Silk, Velvet & Brocade Selections
Royal Heritage

The Ceremonial Prince Suit

Ivory brocade with engraved antique gold crest buttons and breeches.
